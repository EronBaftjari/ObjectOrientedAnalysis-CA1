# Purpose Scope Boundary

## Purpose

The Job Service will give a staff and manager each reliable record of each job , from enquiry to collection
This will address the unclear specifications, job status, inconsistent recording of the customers approval and spending limits
and also hidden material .

its aim is for staff to see the current specification and status of every job and when analysis is done Customer may be informed that more pay is essential,
this will allow the record to be recorded consistently and will allow managers to see shortages of materials, machine loading and the expected time completion before 
the scheduling decisions are made and recorded

## **In Scope Functions**

* Record jobs specifications job status and link it to the customer and job 
* Record the sketches,sample or desciption of faulty item and link it to customer and job
* Send Customer quotation and record if the customer approves it and how it was approved and what item it was for
* if additional faults are found during work, flag it that a customers decision  is needed to continue.
* Record the Materials stock and what Materials are needed
* Record The materials that are being used to a Specific job
* Show machine loading and available machines on jobs
* Record Components available and if collected by customer while remainder remains
* Record Estimation of Job being completed
* Show manager approved jobs, shortages, machine loading, external services, rework  done and expected completion dates
  
## **Out Of Scope Functions**

* CAD Authoring
* payroll
* Puchasing from suppliers and managing full stock inventory of materials
* Record every offcut individually
* Customer self service 

## Dependencies

* Customers supply the descripion,sketches and samples at the start of a job
* External Providers carry out services such as coating and welding
* Customers decide on quotations and the revised quotations given to them
* Suppliers deliver material 


## **Assumptions**

* A job is marked as approved only when customer approval and spending limit is recorded
* Only authorised and expierenced staff can accept the release of a completed job after inspection
* The service records material and the materials that are commmited to jobs
* Components can be collected only when marked as complete
  
## **Constraints**
* Machine have capacity limits, maintenance periods and the restrictions on operators
* Employees have different skills and levels of authorisations to accept completed work
* Staff see specifications but not customer payment details
* Traceability only applies to selected approved jobs,  , offcuts are not recorded but materials used are
