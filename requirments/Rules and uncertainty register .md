
# Rules and Uncertainty

**Business Rules**

| Category | Statement |
|---|-------|
| BR01 | An approved job is not automatically ready to be scheduled |
| BR02 | Finished Components cannot be released to Customer until it has been Approved from inspection |
| BR03 | Each rework must be recorded as caused by either a workshop error or an approved customer change |
| BR04 | Customer must be Contacted before extra work takes place and raises the Spending limit |
| BR05 | Worker can only Carry out a job if they are Qualified |


**Asumptions**
| Category | Statement |
|---|-------|
| AS01 | Every job has an agreed spending limit |
| AS02 | Only inspector can release a completed component |
| AS03 | All staff can access the system during Working hours |
| AS04 | Staff do not need access to customer payment details to carry out their work |
| AS05 | Material is labelled as committed once its been allocated to a job |
| AS06 | Each enquiry includes at least a damaged Item, sample, sketch or description of work|

**Risks**
| Category | Statement |
|---|-------|
| R1 | Sample, sketch or description of work supplied by Customer may be Outdated causing job to be assessed Incorrectly |
| R2 | Staff may record a customer’s spending limit inconsistently, especially when approval is given verbally, causing disputes between the customer and the workshop over cost. |
| R3 | Material may already be commited to 2 jobs leading 1 job to be delayed |
| R4 | Existing job cards, emails and spreadsheets may contain conflicting or incomplete information, causing errors when records are moved into the new system |

**Contraints**
| Category | Statement |
|---|-------|
| C01 | Customer approval must be recordable by email, telephone or signed form | 
| C02 | System can be accessible through website | 
| C03 | System must not require every offcut or material to be recorded individually |
