# StakeHolders


| Stakeholder | Type | Goals | Concerns | Influence | Expected interaction | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Workshop manager | Direct | Track job status, machine loading and material shortages | Approved jobs scheduled before materials, machines or specs are confirmed | High | View job status and reports; schedule jobs | P13, Q1 |
| Inspector | Direct | Record inspection results and decide pass, rework or deviation | Finished work treated as accepted before inspection | High | Record inspection results; release work* | P10, Q4, U5 |
| Machinist | Direct | View current drawings; record operations, measurements and problems | Working from an outdated drawing | Medium | View specs; record operations | P9, Q2 |
| Welder | Direct | View specs and safety notes; record operations and problems | Customer-change rework blamed as workshop error | Medium | View specs; record operations and rework cause | P9, P11, Q6, U6 |
| Stores coordinator | Direct | Track stock and allocate material to jobs | Committed material used for another job | Medium | Record stock; allocate material | P6, Q5, U3 |
| Customer | Indirect | Job done to spec, on time, within agreed price | Extra work exceeding the agreed limit without contact | High | None directly; staff record approvals* | P4, P5, P10, Q3, U1 |
| Accounts / finance staff | Indirect | Accurate job cost records including rework | Rework cost charged to the wrong party | Low | None directly; uses cost records* | P11, P14 |
| Material suppliers | External | Clear orders for job materials | Late orders because shortages are spotted late | Medium | None directly; staff record orders* | P6, P8 |
| External service providers | External | Clear instructions; return components on time | Missing current spec; unclear expected dates | Medium | None directly; staff record send-out and return* | P7, P9, P13 |
