# Scenario Interpretation


Munster Engineering Workshop completes fabrication, machining and repair jobs for farms, businesses, and community organizations. Work ranges from simple component repair to small custom assemblies. 

Present Process: A customer supplies a damaged item,sketches,sample or a description, The staff will then asses the materials and finish operations, But some jobs cannot be fully assessed until it has completely disassembled , A Quotation is then given to the customer and is sent a email ,phone number  or sign a form which then they can choose to approve it .Although throughout the operation a revised quotation can be sent and customer decides again 

Jobs may be Scheduled before a machine, specialist or material is confirmed ,once inspected staff will then record completed operations and will include the materials used and issues that were found, Information is spread across job cards, email and spreadsheets


## Problem & Consequences

| Problem (evidence) | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Staff cannot identify the current specification, job status or reason for delay | Work may proceed without knowing specifications, which can lead to work being done the wrong way |
| Verbal approval and spending limits are recorded inconsistently | Verbal disputes are inconsistent, which can lead to information being lost and work exceeding what was agreed |
| Stock on hand may already be committed to another job | Shortage of materials is known late, so scheduling is then unreliable |
| Job cards lag behind physical work | The manager cannot see true progress, machine loading or completion dates |
| The person who did the work may not have authority to accept it | Work could be released unaccepted and not done right |

# Desired Outcome

 Staff see the current specification and status of every job approval, limits and revisions are recorded consistently.
Material and machine commitments are visible before scheduling; acceptance and release are distinct from manufacture; the manager sees approved jobs, shortages, machine loading, external services, rework and expected completion; workshop staff see specifications and safety notes but not payment details.


**Based on Document** 

| Source | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Workshop manager | A job can be approved but still not ready to schedule |
| Machinist | Drawing version matters more than the date on the job card |
| Customer | Wants contact before extra work pushes cost above the agreed limit |
| Inspector | Finished manufacturing does not mean accepted for release |
| Stores coordinator | Material in the rack may already be committed |
| Welder | Rework from a changed specification differs from correcting workshop error |

**Scope boundary (from scenario):** job coordination and production records are in scope. CAD authoring, machine control, payroll and detailed accounting are out of scope
